## MORE in one paragraph

**A multidisciplinary studio that builds original IP, produces across audio and visual, and turns talent & brands into cultural ventures.** Dubai Production City since 2011, 10,000 sq ft, eight rooms, six capabilities under one roof. *Service pays the bills. Ownership builds the future.*

The commercial reality the agent has to serve (from the strategy doc):

- **Legacy services fund the pivot.** Audio (voice-over and sound design) is the top revenue line and the most profitable one, but it is *not working at capacity*. Video is moving back to mid/high-end work. Studios need utilisation.
- **The future can't stop.** Branded Entertainment, MORE Originals and Ventures need brand partners and sponsors now. Ramadan 2027 budgets lock in Q4 2026.
- **New business is organised by service, not by region or type.** So every lead the agent finds belongs to one **play**, and each play has an owner.

### Proposed launch order

1. **P0 Network reactivation.** Cheapest, warmest, fastest meetings, and the new site is the perfect reason.
2. **P1 Agency audio partner.** Fastest cash, and it fills the studio's spare capacity.
3. **P5 Ramadan sponsorship sprint.** Time-boxed; it must start by mid-November, so it runs as a concierge version (research and drafts) before the full system is live.
4. **P7 Studio bookings** and **P9 Tender radar.** High automation fit and low risk.
5. **P3, P2, P4**, then **P8, P6, P10** once the first plays are tuned.
