# 03 · Outreach playbook: messages, follow-ups, replies and meetings

## What "natural" means here

Generic AI personalisation is burned. Decision-makers get 120+ unsolicited emails a week and delete pattern-matched ones in seconds. What still works is simple: **a real person, a real reason, something useful, and an easy next step.** Every message the agent writes must pass these rules:

1. **It comes from a real person at MORE, in their voice.** The agent learns each sender's tone from their own sent mail (with their permission): greeting style, sign-off, sentence length, how formal they are.
2. **The signal is the personalisation.** One specific, true, recent observation ("VML picked up X's KSA business last week") instead of flattery ("loved your recent post").
3. **Observation → relevance → soft ask, in under 100 words** for a first touch (best campaigns run under 80).
4. **One receipt, chosen for them.** A relevant proof point from the approved list. Not a capabilities dump.
5. **One easy ask.** Send a script, take a 15-minute call, come see the studio, or "want me to send the reel?"
6. **Every follow-up adds something new.** No "just bumping this".
7. **Plain text.** No tracking pixels, no tracked links, no attachments in the first email. Assets are hosted on a MORE page and linked.
8. **Nothing unverifiable.** Every factual claim in a draft must trace to a source in the research dossier. A checker step blocks anything it can't trace.

**Banned:** "I hope this email finds you well", "I came across your profile", "quick question", "synergy", "circle back", fake familiarity, false urgency, invented mutual connections, prices (unless the play allows them), NDA'd clients, emoji in first touches.

**House style:** UK spelling, matching the website. Short sentences. Confident, warm, a little wit. MORE's site voice ("The receipts.", "Your name. Our craft.") is a seasoning, not the script. These are personal notes, not ads.

## Channels

| Channel | Used for | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Email** | Primary first touch and follow-ups | **Always from the sender's own wedomore.com mailbox**, warm or new, with strict daily caps (see 05) |
| **LinkedIn** | Profile view, connection request, follow-up message | **The agent drafts; a human clicks send.** No automation tools on anyone's profile. |
| **WhatsApp** | Warm contacts and anyone who replied or shared a number | **Never cold.** The agent prepares a one-tap draft (wa.me link with the text pre-filled) for the person to send from their own phone. The WhatsApp Business API is only used after opt-in. |
| **Phone** | After a reply, for warm contacts | UAE telemarketing rules apply (9am–6pm, no repeat after a refusal). The agent only schedules reminders; it never dials. |
| **Instagram DM** | Creators (P10) | Human-sent drafts only. |

## Timing

- **UAE:** Tue–Thu, 9–11am GST is best; Mon and early afternoon are fine. **KSA:** Sun–Wed, 9–11am AST. Nothing goes out Friday afternoon, Saturday, or on public holidays.
- **Ramadan 2027 (~8 Feb – 9 Mar):** no new cold sequences during it; greetings and warm nurture only. **The real Ramadan selling window is now through January.**
- **Calendar hooks:** UAE National Day (2 Dec), DICM (Nov), Saudi Founding Day (22 Feb), Eid, season openings (Global Village, Riyadh Season, DSF).
- **Language:** English by default. Arabic or bilingual for KSA senior and government contacts, and for Arabic-content buyers, where naming the dialects we cover is itself a proof point. Arabic drafts always get a native-speaker review before sending.
- **Honorifics:** first names in the UAE agency world; "Mr./Ms." for Saudi senior and government contacts. **Sheikhs and Excellencies are never contacted by the agent.**

## Sequences by play

Default cadence for a new contact (tuned per play):

| Day | Touch |
|---|---|
| 0 | Email 1: observation → relevance → soft ask |
| 1–2 | LinkedIn profile view and connection request (human-sent; short note or none) |
| 5–7 | Email 2: the value-first asset (reel, sketch, one-pager), or a sharper angle |
| 10–12 | LinkedIn message, if they accepted |
| 18–21 | Email 3: a relevant receipt, a direct meeting ask with two time options, and a gracious close-the-loop. Then watchlist: re-enter only on a new signal or after 90 days. |

Follow-ups produce more than half of all replies, but longer sequences raise unsubscribe and complaint rates. Since every email carries wedomore.com's reputation, we cap at **3 emails**, keep each shorter than the last, and stop the moment anything comes back. The owner approves the whole sequence once, up front; follow-ups then go out on schedule unless the contact replies.

### Value-first assets (the hook that's uniquely MORE)

Tier A only, or after a first positive reply. Small, human-made or curated, always framed as a concept (no usage rights):

| Play | Asset |
|---|---|
| P1 Agency audio | A 60-second shortlist of 3–5 roster voices in the right dialect for their client's category (curated from a tagged voice library) |
| P4 Brand sound | Three bullet points on how their recent ads sound, plus one idea; a 5–10 second sonic sketch for top accounts |
| P5 Branded entertainment | One-page show-fit note: audience overlap with The Rahal Show or the slate, and two integration ideas |
| P3 Studio-to-studio | An Atmos test mix offer, or a dialect ADR sample |
| P7 Studios | A studio walkthrough video and an invite to visit (or a first-session offer, if approved) |

Prefer pre-recorded human reads from the roster over AI voices. They show the craft and avoid voice-consent problems.

## Replies: what the agent does with each kind

Every inbound reply is classified within minutes. **Nothing auto-replies until a play has graduated** (see 05). The agent drafts; the owner approves.

| Reply type | Agent action |
|---|---|
| **Wants a meeting** | Proposes 2–3 slots from the owner's calendar (or a studio visit), books on acceptance, notifies the owner, updates the CRM to *Meeting Set* |
| **Interested, wants info** (deck, reel, rates) | Drafts a reply with the right asset; anything about price is routed to the owner |
| **Referral** ("speak to X") | Creates the new contact, drafts a warm intro that names the referrer, and thanks the referrer |
| **Not now / timing** | Thanks them, sets a nurture date they implied ("after Ramadan" → mid-March) |
| **Not interested** | Short, gracious close. Contact is marked closed-lost for this play. |
| **Unsubscribe, or "who gave you my email?"** | Added to suppression immediately and permanently across all mailboxes. One-line confirmation only if appropriate. Owner notified. |
| **Out of office** | Pauses the sequence, resumes the day after their return date; picks up any alternate contact named |
| **Bounce** | Marks the address invalid, tries the next waterfall result, alerts if the domain is risky |
| **Anything sensitive** (complaint, legal, pricing negotiation, an existing client issue) | Escalated to the owner straight away. The agent does not draft. |

**Incoming requests** (a studio booking, a brief, a quote request through the website form or knowmore@wedomore.com) go to Charles too. The agent attaches a quick profile of the sender and which room or service they're asking about, and drafts a reply for him to send.

## Meetings: from "yes" to a prepared call

1. **Propose times.** The agent reads the owner's Google Calendar free/busy and offers 2–3 slots in the recipient's time zone. It respects GST vs AST, the KSA Sun–Thu week, Ramadan hours, and keeps 30-minute buffers.
2. **Default CTA: "come see the studio".** Ten thousand square feet and eight rooms are MORE's best sales tool. The invite includes the address, parking notes and which rooms to see. Google Meet is the fallback, added automatically.
3. **Book.** Calendar invite from the owner's calendar, confirmation in the email thread, reminder the day before.
4. **Prep brief to the owner** two hours before: who they are, the signal, what we sent, what they replied, likely needs, suggested angles, relevant receipts.
5. **After the meeting:** the owner drops a two-line note (or the Gemini meeting notes are picked up). The agent drafts the follow-up and updates the CRM stage.
