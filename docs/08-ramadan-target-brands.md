# 08 · Ramadan 2027 target brands (P5): suggested list for Charles to approve

**What this is for.** Ramadan 2027 is expected around 8 February, and brands lock their Ramadan budgets in Q4. *The Rahal Show* strategy (20 Oct 2026) sets **15 November – mid December** as the sales sprint for the Ramadan Nights presenting partner and the Season 1 title partner. This list is who the agent would research and draft for in that window.

**How it was built**

1. **Categories with no celebrity-guest conflicts** come first. Those are the ones the Rahal strategy marks safe for season-level deals: delivery and e-commerce, tourism, Chinese cars, regional banks, appliances and home, coffee.
2. **Brands that spend heavily on Ramadan** and kept spending through 2026: delivery, telcos, banks, retail, government tourism and entertainment.
3. **Warm links first.** Brands on MORE's website "Trusted by" wall or already working with MORE are flagged ★. They count as warm outreach, not new contacts.
4. **CRM status.** Whether the UAE Brands tab already has a contact at the brand. Names and emails are kept out of this repo.

**Two things to confirm before the sprint**
- **Is MORE selling the Rahal Show's sponsorship inventory?** The Rahal strategy lists "who sells: in-house, MoreComms, or a sales house" as still open. If MORE isn't selling it, the list still works for MORE's own branded-entertainment formats (the "Alt pitch" column).
- **The 40 a week cap.** Under the balanced mix, P5 gets about 4–5 new contacts a week, so roughly 20 of the non-warm brands get a first email before mid December. The ★ warm brands go out on top of that. To reach every brand below in time, P5 would need a temporary boost to about 10 a week during the sprint. Your call.
