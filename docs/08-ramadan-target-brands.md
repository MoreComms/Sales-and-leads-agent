# 08 · Ramadan 2027 target brands (P5): approved by Charles, 21 Oct

**What this is for.** Ramadan 2027 is expected around 8 February, and brands lock their Ramadan budgets in Q4. These 43 brands are where P5 (Branded Entertainment) looks first. The pitch is **MORE making Ramadan content the brand owns**: a short branded series, a food-entertainment format from the Flame kitchen studio, a podcast, or the Ramadan campaign itself (film, social, Arabic VO and music, sonic identity).

**No sponsorship pitching.** The Rahal Show hasn't launched, so nothing here sells show sponsorship (decided 21 Oct).

**How it runs.** P5 takes its normal share of the balanced slate (about 5 new contacts a week; no boost, decided 21 Oct), so roughly 20 of the non-warm brands get a first email before the Ramadan budget window closes. The highest-scoring go first. The 8 warm brands (★, past or current MORE clients) go out as reactivation emails on top of that.

**Columns**
- **Pitch:** what MORE would offer for Ramadan.
- **CRM:** whether the UAE Brands tab already has a contact. Names and emails are kept out of this repo.

★ = past or current MORE client (warm).
