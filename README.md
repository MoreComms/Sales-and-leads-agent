# MORE · Sales & Leads Agent

An automated pipeline that finds the right people for MORE's services, researches them, writes outreach in the voice of the person sending it, follows up, reads the replies and books meetings for the MORE team to take forward.

**Status: planning.** Nothing is built yet. This repository holds the plan to agree before we build and deploy.

## The idea in six lines

1. **Warm first, then signals, never generic lists.** Start with 15 years of past clients and dormant contacts (the new website is the reason to reconnect). Then go after accounts where something just happened that creates a need: an agency won an account, a brand is planning Ramadan, an RFQ dropped, a production is coming to the UAE.
2. **Organised by service, as the strategy asks.** Eleven *plays* map MORE's six capabilities to the website's five doors (brands, agencies, studios, creators, investors). Each has a buyer, its signals, a hook, a call to action and an owner.
3. **Sounds like a person, because a person sends it.** Every email goes from the sender's own wedomore.com mailbox. No extra domains. Short, specific, true, one easy ask, often "come see the studio". Claude researches and drafts; the owner approves every first touch from their phone.
4. **Few, hand-picked, high-potential leads.** Because everything goes out under MORE's real domain, the agent only contacts people with a strong fit and a fresh reason. That's roughly 100–200 new people a month across the team, at human volumes. Everyone else is watched, not emailed.
5. **From reply to meeting, handled.** Replies are classified within minutes, times proposed from the owner's calendar, invites sent, and a prep brief lands before the call. The MORE – CRM sheet stays the team's view and updates itself.
6. **Safe by design.** Strict sending caps and an automatic pause protect wedomore.com. Opt-outs are global and permanent. No cold WhatsApp, no LinkedIn bots, no invented facts. UAE-first pending a legal check.
