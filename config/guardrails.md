# Guardrails (hard rules)

These are not suggestions. If following a task would break one, skip the task and note why in the Log and the summary.

## Who may be contacted

- **Never contact:**
  - anyone on the Suppression tab (email or whole domain);
  - Sheikhs, anyone styled His or Her Excellency, investors;
  - anyone Charles marks "personal" in Settings.
- **Never draft to a personal address:** gmail.com, googlemail.com, hotmail.*, outlook.com, live.com, yahoo.*, icloud.com, me.com, aol.com, proton.me, protonmail.com. Find a work address or make it a "LinkedIn it" note.
- **Collision check before every first draft:**
  1. Search Charles's Gmail for the address and the company domain over the last 90 days (`from:` and `to:`). Any thread involving someone @wedomore.com means skip, and mention it to Charles if the signal is strong.
  2. Check MORE – CRM: if the person or company has *WHO'S LEADING?* = Daniel Sheridan, Joanne or Elsa Afif with any interaction since June 2026, skip and surface the signal to Charles instead ("Omar's agency just won X; Daniel's contact, worth a nudge?").
- **Geography:** UAE first. Kuwait and Qatar are allowed for P1 agency audio. **No cold email to Saudi Arabia or Germany** (warm contacts Charles already knows are fine, as P0). International production houses are allowed for P3 only.

## How many

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| New first-touch drafts | ≤ 10 a day, ≤ 40 a week (Monday–Sunday), across all plays except P0 |
| Reactivation drafts (P0) | ≤ 5 a day |
| Unsent agent drafts waiting | If more than 15 are waiting, create only half the usual new drafts and say so in the summary |
| Emails per sequence | 3 maximum (email 1 by Charles, follow-ups 2 and 3 by you) |
| Follow-up spacing | Email 2: 5–7 working days after email 1. Email 3: 7–10 working days after email 2. |

## When

- **Working days:** Monday to Friday, Dubai time. Skip UAE public holidays (check the holidays calendar).
- **Follow-ups are sent only 09:30–11:30 Dubai time, Monday to Thursday.** A follow-up due on a Friday moves to the next Monday.
- **Ramadan 2027 (about 8 Feb – 9 Mar):** no new first-touch drafts to new contacts. Reactivations, replies and follow-ups already running continue, in a gentler tone. Greetings are allowed.
- **Before Eid and National Day (2 Dec):** keep going, but don't schedule follow-ups on the holiday itself.

## What may be said

- **Prices:** never. If asked, flag it to Charles: "pricing question, your call".
- **Clients and projects:** only those marked *free to name* in `config/proof.md`. Others go into the summary as options.
- **Facts:** every claim about the recipient or their company must come from a source read in this run (URL or email). Put the sources in the sheet.
- **Familiarity:** mention a past interaction only if it exists in Charles's Gmail, the CRM or the ledger.
- **Promises:** never commit MORE to dates, deliverables, discounts, free work or exclusivity.
- **Banned phrases:** "I hope this email finds you well", "I came across your profile", "quick question", "synergy", "touch base", "just bumping this", "I wanted to reach out", "revolutionise", "game-changer", false urgency, emoji in first emails.

## Format

- Plain text. No tracking, no attachments in email 1. Links only from the list in `config/proof.md`: a deck plus wedomore.com for agencies and branded entertainment, wedomore.com alone for everyone else.
- Email 1: under 100 words. Follow-ups: shorter than the email before.
- Spelling and punctuation follow `config/voice-charles.md` (consistent within an email). Arabic only if Charles enables it for a lead.
- Drafts are created with Charles's Gmail signature (`includeSignature: true`).

## Replies you must not answer on your own

Escalate to Charles straight away (alert email, no draft):
- complaints;
- legal or data questions ("where did you get my email?" also gets added to Suppression);
- pricing negotiation;
- anything about an existing client project or invoice;
- anything sensitive or personal.

## Sending that you may do yourself

- **Follow-ups 2 and 3** in threads where Charles sent email 1, only if:
  - nobody replied (check the full thread first);
  - the contact isn't suppressed;
  - the follow-up text was written and logged when the sequence was created (or is a light date update of it).
- **Summary and alert emails to charles@wedomore.com only.**
- Nothing else. Meeting confirmations and scheduling replies are drafts until Charles turns on automatic scheduling (Settings: `auto_scheduling = on`).
