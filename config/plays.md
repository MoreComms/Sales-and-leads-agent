# Plays (run-ready)

A play = one offer, one buyer, the signals that say "now", one hook, one ask. Full reasoning is in `docs/01-what-more-sells.md` and `docs/03-outreach-playbook.md`.

**Balance rule (decided 21 Oct):** no play outranks another.
- Each week the 40 new-contact slots are shared evenly across the active email plays **P1–P8** (5 each).
- A play's unused slots go to the strongest waiting leads in other plays. Never fill a slot with a weak lead.
- **P0** runs alongside (up to 5 a day). **P9** and **P10** never produce emails.
- Active plays and any boosts are in the Settings tab.

## Every first email follows this shape

1. **Observation:** one specific, true, recent thing about them, from a source you read. That's the reason you're writing *now*.
2. **Relevance:** one line on why that matters for something MORE does, plus at most one receipt from `config/proof.md`.
3. **Soft ask:** one easy next step: send a script, a 15-minute call, come see the studio, "want me to send the reel?"

Rules:
- Under 100 words, plain text, written as Charles (see `config/voice-charles.md`).
- Subject: 2–6 words, lower-key, specific to them (e.g. "[Brand] KSA voices", "Founder interviews, filmed properly"). No clickbait.
- Follow-up 2 adds something new: the asset, a sharper angle or a relevant receipt.
- Follow-up 3 is a short, gracious close with two meeting options. Both are written when the sequence is created and stored in the Sequences tab.

## P0 · Network reactivation
- **Who:** people Charles has worked with or pitched, gone quiet for 60+ days. Sources: the reactivation list in the Leads tab, the 2025 ledger, Charles's Gmail, CRM rows led by Charles.
- **Now:** it's been a while, plus something genuinely new at MORE that fits *them*, or their own news (new role, launch, win). **Never use the new website's launch as the reason.**
- **Hook:** a personal note referencing the real last project or conversation, plus one new thing that fits them. For agency contacts, the agencies deck link is fine. (MORE Sound for an agency producer, the podcast sets for a comms lead, the Atmos room for a post house, the kitchen studio for an F&B brand).
- **Ask:** coffee at Production City, or a catch-up call.
- **Tone:** warmer and shorter than cold emails. No pitch paragraph.
- **Sequence:** email 1 only, plus one gentle nudge after 10 working days if no reply.
